CHAPTER 01

Before You Start

Five minutes of reading here will save you from the most common and costly mistakes.

◆What is VelocityX?

VelocityX is a website (and phone app) that helps you buy and sell crypto coins, Indian stocks, forex and options. It has a built-in AI (artificial intelligence) that studies the market every few minutes and tells you whether it is a good time to buy, sell or wait.

You can trade in two ways:

✋ By yourself

You look at the AI's advice and click Buy or Sell yourself in the AI Terminal.

🤖 With a bot

You create a "bot" — a robot helper. It watches the market 24 hours a day and trades for you, following safety rules.

◆Paper mode vs Live mode

📝 Paper (practice)

Fake wallet with fake money. Perfect for learning. You can never lose real money here. Always start here.

💰 Live (real money)

Uses the exchange account you connected. Profits and losses are real. Only use after practising in Paper mode.

You switch between them with the Paper / Live button at the top of the screen. When Live is on, a yellow warning bar is shown on every page so you never forget.

◆The 6 golden safety rules

  1. Practise first. Use Paper mode for at least 1–2 weeks before using real money.
  2. Only use money you can afford to lose. Trading always has risk. No AI can promise profit.
  3. Never turn on "Withdrawals" when you make exchange API keys. VelocityX never needs it.
  4. Never share your password, API secret or 2FA code with anyone — not even support staff.
  5. Turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your VelocityX account and on your exchange.
  6. Know the Kill Switch. The red Kill Switch button at the top stops everything instantly in an emergency.

CHAPTER 03

Connect Your Exchange

To trade with real money, VelocityX needs a "key" from your exchange. Each customer uses their own key, so trades happen only in your own account.

What is an API key? Think of it as a special door key you give to VelocityX. It can place trades for you, but — if you set it up as shown below — it can never withdraw or move your money out. You make two things: an API Key (like a username) and an API Secret (like a password).

AMake your API key on Binance

  1. Sign in to binance.com (on a computer is easiest). Your Binance account must be verified (KYC).
  2. Click your profile icon → API Management.
  3. Click Create API → choose System generated → give it a name like VelocityX.
  4. Finish the security check (email / phone / authenticator code).
  5. You now see the API Key and Secret Key. Copy both and keep them safe. The Secret is shown only once.
  6. Click Edit restrictions. Tick Enable Spot & Margin Trading. Make sure Enable Withdrawals is OFF.
  7. Recommended: choose Restrict access to trusted IPs only and enter the server IP your platform owner gives you. Click Save.
Never tick "Enable Withdrawals". Never send your Secret Key on WhatsApp, email or phone to anyone.
Want to practise with Binance first? Make free test keys at testnet.binance.vision. These use fake money on Binance's own test system.

BAdd the key in VelocityX

  1. In the left menu click Account & Security.
  2. Open the Exchange connections tab.
  3. In Exchange choose Binance. (Bybit, OKX and Zerodha are also supported. OKX also needs a Passphrase.)
  4. Paste your API key and API secret.
  5. Testnet / demo account tick box: tick it only if you made test keys. Untick it for real Binance keys.
  6. Click Save connection. Your secret is locked (encrypted) and never shown again.
  7. Click Test next to the new connection. You should see "Connected" with your number of assets.
Zerodha (NSE stocks, forex and options): add your Kite Connect API key and secret the same way, then click Log in to Kite. Kite logins expire every morning around 6 AM, so log in again each trading day.

◧AI Terminal — check a coin and trade by hand

What it isA price chart, the AI's advice and a Buy/Sell box.
Use it whenYou want to study one coin or place a trade yourself.

Get the AI's advice

  1. Click AI Terminal. Click the coin name at the top-left and pick or search a coin (e.g. BTC).
  2. Choose a timeframe (15m, 1h, 4h, 1d). Bigger timeframes = slower, calmer signals.
  3. Click Run AI Analysis.
  4. Read the result: BUY, SELL or WAIT, the probability, the Market Regime (type of market), the suggested Stop-loss and Take-profit, and the Key Drivers (reasons).
  5. Look at Risk Manager / Safety gates: if it says Rejected, the safety system does not allow this trade now.

Place a trade yourself

  1. Check the top bar: Paper for practice, Live for real money.
  2. In the Place Order box choose Buy or Sell.
  3. Type the amount, or tap 25% / 50% / 75% / 100% of your balance.
  4. Click the Buy/Sell button and confirm. Your trade appears in Trade History.
The chart has extra lines you can turn on or off: EMA lines (trend), Bollinger bands, support/resistance levels and a colour strip that shows the market type.

◎AI Scanner — find the best coins today

What it isThe AI checks 100+ coins and lists the best chances.
Use it whenYou don't know which coin to trade.
  1. Click AI Scanner. Choose a timeframe and how many coins to check (100 or 150).
  2. Click Scan Market and wait about a minute.
  3. The Filter Funnel shows how many coins passed each check (enough trading, right movement, good trend, AI score, safety).
  4. Top Setups lists the best coins with their Confidence, Quality grade (A is best) and strategy. Click a coin to open it in the Terminal.

◉Market Intel

CHAPTER 05

Trading Bots

A bot is your robot trader. It watches the market day and night and follows strict safety rules.

🤖Create your first bot

  1. Click Bot Manager, then New Bot.
  2. Bot name: any name, e.g. My BTC Bot.
  3. Exchange and Trading pair: e.g. Binance and BTCUSDT. Any coin on the exchange is allowed.
  4. Timeframe: how often the bot thinks. 1h or 4h is best for beginners.
  5. AI strategy: choose AI Adaptive (recommended — it changes its plan when the market changes).
  6. Mode: Paper to practise. Choose Live only when you are ready.
  7. Leave the safety numbers as they are for now (see the table below).
  8. Click Create bot, then click Start on the bot card.

🧭How AI Adaptive changes its plan

Every new candle (time period) the AI decides what type of market it is and picks the right plan:

Market typeWhat the bot does
📈 Bull Trend (going up steadily)Uses the Trend plan — buys and rides the move up.
🚀 Breakout / High volatilityUses the Breakout plan with a smaller amount.
↔️ Sideways / Low volatilityUses Mean Reversion — buys low, sells high inside the range.
📉 Bear Trend / Breakdown / PanicDoes not buy. It waits safely until the market is healthy again.
No strategy wins every trade. Losing trades are normal. The goal is that winners are bigger than losers over time. Profit is never guaranteed.

📋Reading the bot card

  • A green border means the bot is running. Buttons: Stop, Start / Resume, Logs, edit ✎ and delete 🗑.
  • Total PnL, Win rate, and Last signal (what the AI decided last and why).
  • When the bot holds a coin you see Avg entry (buy price), Stop-loss and Take-profit. Click Close position to sell now.
  • The top row shows Bots running, Today realized, Exposure (money in trades) and Drawdown (drop from your best balance).
  • Logs show every step in plain words: info, trade, profit, loss / error.

∑Other bot types

DCA bot

Buys a small fixed amount every few candles (e.g. "Buy every N candles"), then sells when the average price is up by your Take profit on avg cost %. Good for long-term saving.

Grid bot

Works in sideways markets: places many small buy-low / sell-high orders inside a price range. It closes itself if the price falls out of the range.

CHAPTER 07

Test & Learn

Test ideas on past data before risking money, ask the AI questions, and learn from every trade.

⚗Backtest Lab — test before you trade

What it isRuns a strategy on years of past prices to see how it would have done.
Use it whenBefore you start any new bot.
  1. Click Backtest Lab.
  2. Pick up to 5 Coins. Want a coin not shown? Type it in the search box and click Add.
  3. Pick up to 4 Timeframes and a Strategy.
  4. Leave the other numbers as they are (they include real fees and slippage) and click Run Backtest.
  5. Read the results (table below). Check the Trades, Walk-forward, Monte Carlo and By regime tabs for more detail.
ResultWhat is good
Avg out-of-sample returnAbove 0% — it made money on data it never trained on.
Profitable out-of-sampleThe more markets profitable, the better.
Probability of lossLower is better (from Monte Carlo — thousands of "what if" runs).
Win rate / Profit factorProfit factor above 1.2 is good.
Good past results do not promise future profit, but bad past results are a strong warning. Never go Live with a strategy that loses in the backtest.

📓Trade Journal

Every finished trade is saved automatically with notes: why it was opened, the market type, and the best and worst price during the trade. At the top, AI Insights and Most Common Mistakes show patterns in your losing trades, so you can avoid them next time.

The AI keeps checking itself: is it as accurate as it claims? Here you can Train New Model Candidate on chosen markets, compare results in the Model Registry, and promote a better model only if it passes the Promotion gates (safety tests). Most users can leave this page alone.

CHAPTER 08

Safety & Your Account

Control your risk, stop everything in an emergency, and keep your account secure.

🛡Risk Manager

  • Risk Limits: set the rules every bot must follow — max exposure, daily loss limit, minimum signal quality and more.
  • Emergency Protection: the system pauses bots on its own after repeated exchange errors, stale data or abnormal prices.
  • System Health: exchange connection, speed (latency) and last check time.
  • Live stats: Running bots, Realized today, Exposure and Drawdown from peak.

⛔The Kill Switch — emergency stop

Use it if the market crashes, something looks wrong, or you are not sure. Pressing it will: stop all bots, cancel open orders, close your positions, and turn off auto-trading.
  1. Click the red Kill Switch at the top-right (also on the Risk Manager page).
  2. Confirm. A red bar "Auto-trading halted" appears on every page.
  3. When it is safe again, click Resume in the same place.

🧾Trade History

A full list of every trade, with filters All / Paper / Live. At the top: Total trades, Win rate, Realized PnL and Fees paid. Useful for your records and taxes.
